# Welcome to My-PI 🧠

This is your **private second brain** — a local LLM Wiki that never leaves your
machine. Everything here is plain Markdown, so your notes are yours forever.

## How it works
- Write notes in Markdown and link them with `[[wiki-links]]` like [[Graph View]].
- Add `#tags` or a `tags:` frontmatter line to group ideas.
- Watch connections form in the [[Graph View]].
- Ask questions with [[RAG Query]] and generate new notes with [[Note Generation]].

## Privacy
Your notes live only in the `vault/` folder on your disk. The server binds to
`127.0.0.1`, and the whole `vault/` is git-ignored — nothing is ever published.

See also: [[DSPy Modules]]
